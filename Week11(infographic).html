Fish for Plastic 🎣

Pay the world's fishermen to catch plastic instead of fish.

Arjun Aujla · Fall 2026

The problem: two broken commons

30 Mt
of plastic already in the ocean, plus 1.7 Mt more every year
Nobody owns it. It's worth less than the cost of picking it up, so nobody does.
$22B
a year in harmful subsidies paying fleets to overfish
35.5% of fish stocks are overfished. Taxpayers fund the race to empty the sea.
Aluminum sells for ~$1.72/kg. PET plastic sells for ~$0.34/kg. That's why cans get picked up and plastic floats. The trash problem is a price problem.

☀️ My first idea, launching trash into the sun: ~$46 trillion a year, about 40% of world GDP. Fish for Plastic costs about 0.1% of that.

Why it's not that crazy

  • Germany's €0.25 bottle deposit: 98% returned
  • Plastic Bank already pays the poor per kilo of plastic
  • Scotland's Fishing for Litter: ~480 boats landing trash
  • WTO fishing-subsidy deal in force since Sept 2025

Risks & fixes

  • Cobra effect: land bounty < cost of new plastic
  • Ocean-washing fraud: GPS-tracked boats + weathering audits
  • No global treaty: "plastic club" with border adjustments
  • Fish prices: transition pay while stocks rebuild
